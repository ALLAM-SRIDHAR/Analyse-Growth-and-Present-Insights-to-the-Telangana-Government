# Telangana Growth Analysis

## Concept of the Project

Telangana is one of the fastest growing states in India and a state with an open data policy. This project focuses on analyzing the growth of Telangana across various sectors and providing valuable insights to the Telangana Government. The goal is to help the government make data-informed decisions that further support the state's growth.

## Problem Statement

The objective of this project is to provide insights to the Telangana Government to help them make data-informed decisions that will support the continued growth of the state.

## Secondary Research Questions

1. **What are the top 5 districts to buy commercial properties in Telangana?**  
   Justify the answer based on the analysis.
   
2. **What significant policies or initiatives were put into effect by the current government to enhance economic growth, investments, and employment in Telangana?**  
   Can we quantify the impact of these policies using available data?
   
3. **Provide top 5 insights and 5 recommendations to the Telangana government for sustained growth in the next 5 years**  
   These insights should be based on the analysis performed.

## Project Objective

- Explore datasets from **Stamp Registration**, **Transportation**, and **TS-iPASS** and understand their attributes, categories, and time periods.
- Analyze trends and patterns within each department.
- Identify growth opportunities and areas needing attention.
- Identify correlations among these departments and report on the overall growth of the state through insights and visuals such as shape maps.
- Present all these insights in a comprehensive presentation.
